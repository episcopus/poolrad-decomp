## Issues found in the research files (outside the guide, for the record)

- 05:290 says `$FF9D/$FF9E` are in 16-byte units; MAME and 02 say 8-byte units (16-byte steps are only Sock's safety advice).
- 01:5 and 01:120 use NTSC broadcast timing (59.94 Hz, 113.75 cycles per line); the GIME runs 912 dots per line, 262 lines, 59.92 Hz, 114 cycles per line (02:298 to 299, MAME).
- 06:11 says a DECB disk side is "157.5 KB"; it is 156,672 bytes (153 KiB).
- 02:12 and 02:81 do not mention that MAME only emulates the 1986 timer reload (`GIME_TYPE_1987` is 0).
