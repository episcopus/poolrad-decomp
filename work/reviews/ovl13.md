- [x] must-fix ShowAttackResult (ovl13:02F9): the fourth parameter is named `SpellFlag` and described as "when SpellFlag is set, Target cannot cast". It is the damage applied: the body calls `ApplyDamage(Target, SpellFlag)` (res12 `ApplyDamage(Who; Damage: Byte)`), `ResolveCombatantAttacks` passes `DamageAmount` there (ovl13:1733) and the helpless branch passes `Target^.Flag11B + 5` (current hit points plus 5, so the target dies). The cannot-cast and lose-a-spell part is the consequence of any damage above 0. Rename to what it is (for example `DamageDealt`, with `Damage` the number displayed, which differs: the helpless hit shows 1), and fix the routine comment and the QUIRK text in ResolveCombatantAttacks ("second and third bytes") to say which arguments carry the damage. (fixed in 4b258a6)
- [x] must-fix ComputeMovement (ovl13:0123) and the constant `MaxMovement`: both comments say the Movement is "clamped to 1..$60" / "capped at this". The code is `if (Moves < 1) or (Moves > MaxMovement) then Moves := 1`: a value above $60 becomes 1, not $60. A port from the comment would give a fast combatant the maximum instead of the slowest move. Say so and mark it a `QUIRK` at the clamp (ovl13:0155..0165 by the constant's own comment); the whitepaper has no entry for it either. (fixed in 4b258a6)

Rewrite the sentence from the code, and say what `Combat^.Pad8` is in game terms (it is 0 at round start, 1 once ResolveCombatantAttacks ran: "has attacked this round"), since the condition is unreadable without it. (fixed in 4b258a6)
